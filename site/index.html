  1. Question, entity, target date

    As of October 4, 2026, who is the legal owner of The Washington Post, what is the chain up to the ultimate parent, who controls it, and who is the publisher and CEO?

  2. Documents obtained

    The bounded workflow ran 3 fixed searches and kept 15 documents. Only one sentence in them names WP Company LLC: the 2013 purchase agreement (dated August 5, 2013), which lists it among the seller's subsidiaries, before the sale. The brand is tied to Nash Holdings; the company that publishes it is not. Native search ran 3 searches in each of its three passes.

  3. Answers produced

    Blank, 3 of 3 passes. The parent given as legal owner, 3 of 3. WP Company LLC, 3 of 3.

  4. First point where the error is observable

    In the dossier, before any model reads it: no retrieved document dated after 2013 names the legal entity behind the paper. From there the two models part ways. One declines to answer; the other promotes the parent to owner and cites 2013 coverage of the sale.

  5. Interventions tested

    A different model on the identical dossier. An extraction step told to follow the chain upward, on the identical documents. Native search instead of the workflow.

  6. Result

    Changing the model named the parent as legal owner instead of leaving a blank. Changing the extraction changed nothing. Native search returned WP Company LLC every time, citing an amicus brief filed October 6, 2025; the passage is in the document:

    "WP Company LLC d/b/a The Washington Post is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Nash Holdings LLC"

    Amicus brief, October 6, 2025, read October 4, 2026

  7. What was not corrected

    A targeted search for this one missing link was not run: this edition makes no new paid call. The workflow's searches still never ask for the publishing entity. Native search is a whole recipe, so this run cannot say which part of it found the brief: cause not observable. Our own record leans on 2013 filings for this link.

All eight outlets, three passes each

A pass counts as complete only when owner, parent, controller and leader all match the record with a located passage, or are left blank where the record itself is not established. Located means: the passage is in the cited document, names the entity, states the relation and is not cut before a qualifier. Whether a source is current is not checked mechanically.
